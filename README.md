🍔 Food Delivery Website

 📌 Project Description
The "Food Delivery Website" is a web-based application designed to make online food ordering simple, convenient, and user-friendly. Users can explore food items, browse menus, add products to their cart, and place food orders online.

The application provides an interactive interface that connects customers with a convenient online food ordering experience.

 🎯 Project Objective
To develop a user-friendly online food ordering platform.
To simplify the food ordering process.
To provide an easy way to browse food items and menus.
To manage cart items and customer orders efficiently.
To improve the overall online food delivery experience.

✨ Features
User Interface: Clean, responsive, and attractive design.
Food Menu:Browse different food items and categories.
Food Details: View food information and prices.
Shopping Cart: Add, remove, and update food items.
Order Management: Place and manage food orders.
User Authentication: Secure registration and login (if implemented).
Responsive Design :Supports desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

⚙️ Application Workflow

1. Users open the Food Delivery Website.
2. Browse available food items.
3. Select their preferred food.
4. Add items to the shopping cart.
5. Review the cart and total price.
6. Proceed to checkout.
7. Place the order.
